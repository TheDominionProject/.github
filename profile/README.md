# The Dominion Project

*Work harder to think smarter. Think smarter, not harder.*

Hey, welcome in!

We're a small group who build things to make people's lives a bit easier. That's pretty much the whole idea. If something's broken, annoying, or just doesn't work for the people who need it, we'd rather have a go at fixing it than sit around complaining about it.

## What we work on

- **Games.** Making them, modding them, and building the things that go around them.
- **Music tools.** Stuff for people who make music and want their workflow to be easy. Big shout out to [Flo Tools](https://flotools.org), [OSARA](https://github.com/jcsteh/osara) and VirtualDJ Accessibility.
- **Whatever else comes up.** If it makes someone's day easier, it's fair game. Honestly, we're too lazy to take the long way round, so we make our own shortcuts.

## Where we're at

We're pretty new, so it's a bit quiet round here for now. A lot of what we're working on is still behind closed doors, but public projects will start turning up here once they're ready.
